# Compliance Disclaimer

IdentityForge is educational decision-support software. It helps teams inspect synthetic identity and access data, calculate risk evidence and prioritize human review.

It does **not**:

- Certify HIPAA, GDPR, PCI DSS, ISO 27001 or any other compliance status.
- Provide legal advice.
- Replace access reviews performed by accountable owners.
- Guarantee that detected findings are complete or accurate.
- Perform unauthorized privilege escalation.

All bundled data is synthetic. Users must not upload real patient records, production credentials, real tokens or sensitive personal data into this demonstration workspace.

Findings, anomaly scores and Zero Trust scores require interpretation by qualified reviewers before any operational decision.
